The hardest part of any project is the last 20%. The exciting bit is long gone. The hard architectural decisions are made. What's left is the grinding work of making it actually good.

Most people abandon things here. They call it "done enough" or start something new. I used to do this constantly.

What changed: I started treating that last stretch as the actual product. The rest is just setup.
