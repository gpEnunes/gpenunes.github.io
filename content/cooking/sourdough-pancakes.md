---
title: Sourdough Pancakes
description: "What to do with sourdough discard: make the best pancakes of your life."
date: 2026-03-22
image: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1655890193532-3f51318b23c0?w=800&q=80
time: 25 min
servings: 8
tags: [Breakfast, Sourdough, Brunch]
ingredients:
  - 200g sourdough discard
  - 120ml whole milk
  - 1 egg
  - 1 tbsp sugar
  - 1 tsp baking powder
  - Pinch of salt
  - Butter for frying
  - Maple syrup and fresh fruit to serve
instructions:
  - Whisk together the sourdough discard, milk and egg until smooth and combined.
  - Add sugar, baking powder and salt. Stir to combine — don't overmix or they'll be tough.
  - Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat with a small knob of butter.
  - Pour circles of batter (about 3 tbsp each) into the pan without crowding it.
  - Cook until bubbles form on the surface and edges look set, about 2 minutes.
  - Flip and cook for 1 more minute until golden on the other side.
  - Serve immediately with maple syrup, butter, or fresh fruit.
---

Sourdough discard is gold. Most people throw it away. These pancakes are the reason you should never do that.
