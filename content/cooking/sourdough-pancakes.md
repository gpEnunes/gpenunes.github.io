Sourdough discard is gold. Don't throw it away. Make these instead.

## Ingredients

- 200g sourdough discard
- 120ml milk
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- Pinch of salt
- Butter for frying

## Instructions

1. Whisk together the sourdough discard, milk and egg until smooth.
2. Add sugar, baking powder and salt. Stir to combine — don't overmix.
3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat with a small knob of butter.
4. Pour small circles of batter (about 3 tbsp each) into the pan.
5. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and edges look set, about 2 minutes.
6. Flip and cook for 1 minute more.
7. Serve with maple syrup, butter, or fresh fruit.
