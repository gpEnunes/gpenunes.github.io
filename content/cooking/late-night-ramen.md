Sometimes the best meals are the ones you make at midnight with whatever's in the fridge.

## Ingredients

- 1 pack instant ramen noodles
- 2 eggs (soft-boiled)
- Soy sauce, sesame oil, chilli flakes
- Spring onions, nori

## Instructions

1. Soft-boil the eggs for exactly 6 minutes then transfer to cold water immediately.
2. Build the broth: heat 400ml water with 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil and a splash of mirin.
3. Add the noodles and cook for 2 minutes until just tender.
4. Transfer noodles to a bowl and pour over the broth.
5. Peel and halve the eggs, place on top with spring onions, nori and chilli flakes.
6. Eat immediately.
