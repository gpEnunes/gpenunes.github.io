---
title: Late Night Ramen
description: A quick, satisfying ramen for when it's midnight and you need something warm.
date: 2026-04-15
image: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1655890193532-3f51318b23c0?w=800&q=80
time: 20 min
servings: 1
tags: [Japanese, Noodles, Quick]
ingredients:
  - 1 pack instant ramen noodles
  - 2 eggs
  - 2 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - Splash of mirin
  - 400ml water
  - Spring onions, nori, chilli flakes to serve
instructions:
  - Soft-boil the eggs for exactly 6 minutes then transfer immediately to cold water.
  - Heat 400ml water in a small pot. Add soy sauce, sesame oil and mirin and bring to a simmer.
  - Add the ramen noodles and cook for 2 minutes until just tender.
  - Transfer noodles to a bowl and pour over the broth.
  - Peel and halve the eggs, place on top with spring onions, nori and chilli flakes.
  - Eat immediately while it's still steaming.
---

Sometimes the best meals are the ones you make at midnight with whatever's in the fridge. No plan, no recipe, just instinct.
