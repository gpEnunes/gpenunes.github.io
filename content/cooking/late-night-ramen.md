Sometimes the best meals are the ones you make at midnight with whatever's in the fridge.

## Ingredients

- 1 pack instant ramen noodles
- 2 eggs (soft-boiled)
- Soy sauce, sesame oil, chilli flakes
- Spring onions, nori

## Method

Soft-boil the eggs for exactly 6 minutes, then cold water. Build the broth with soy, sesame oil and a splash of mirin. Cook the noodles, assemble, add toppings. Eat immediately.
