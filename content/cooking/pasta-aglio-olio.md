This is what Italian grandmothers make when they want something fast. There is no simpler, better pasta.

## Ingredients

- 200g spaghetti
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 80ml good olive oil
- 1 tsp dried chilli flakes
- Large handful flat-leaf parsley
- Salt, parmesan to finish

## Instructions

1. Cook spaghetti in heavily salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve 200ml pasta water before draining.
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat.
3. Add garlic and cook slowly until golden (not brown), about 4 minutes.
4. Add chilli flakes and stir for 30 seconds.
5. Add drained pasta and a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously for 2 minutes.
6. Remove from heat, add parsley and parmesan. Serve immediately.
