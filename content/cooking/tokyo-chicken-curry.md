---
title: Tokyo Chicken Curry
description: Japanese curry is its own thing — sweeter, thicker, more comforting than you expect.
date: 2026-04-01
image: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1655890193532-3f51318b23c0?w=800&q=80
time: 45 min
servings: 4
tags: [Japanese, Curry, Comfort Food]
ingredients:
  - 600g chicken thighs, cut into pieces
  - 2 onions, sliced
  - 2 carrots, chopped
  - 2 potatoes, cubed
  - 1 pack S&B Golden Curry roux blocks
  - 700ml chicken stock
  - Oil, salt and pepper
  - Steamed white rice to serve
instructions:
  - Brown the chicken in oil in a large pot over high heat. Season with salt and pepper then set aside.
  - In the same pot, cook onions on medium heat for 10 minutes until deep golden.
  - Add carrots and potatoes and cook for 3 minutes.
  - Return chicken to pot. Add stock and bring to a boil.
  - Reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  - Break curry roux into the pot and stir until completely dissolved and smooth.
  - Simmer for 5 more minutes until thick and glossy. Serve over steamed white rice.
---

Japanese curry is nothing like Indian curry. It's milder, sweeter, and built for long winter evenings when you want something that feels like a hug.
