Japanese curry is nothing like Indian curry. It's milder, sweeter, and built for winter evenings.

## Ingredients

- 600g chicken thighs, cut into pieces
- 2 onions, sliced
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 potatoes, cubed
- 1 pack S&B Golden Curry roux blocks
- 700ml chicken stock
- Oil, salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Brown the chicken in oil in a large pot over high heat. Season with salt and pepper. Set aside.
2. In the same pot, cook onions on medium heat for 10 minutes until golden.
3. Add carrots and potatoes and cook for 3 minutes.
4. Return chicken to pot. Add stock and bring to a boil.
5. Reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes until vegetables are tender.
6. Break curry roux into pot and stir until completely dissolved.
7. Simmer for 5 more minutes until thick and glossy.
8. Serve over steamed white rice.
