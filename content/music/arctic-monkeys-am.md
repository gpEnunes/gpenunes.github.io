AM came out in 2013 and it sounded like confidence made audible. Every riff, every vocal choice, every beat — it all landed like the band knew exactly what they were doing.

That's the rarest thing in any creative work. Most people are figuring it out. AM sounds like the figuring-out is done.

Do I Wanna Know might be the best opening track of the decade.
