OK Computer is a record about machines taking over. Thom Yorke wrote it in the 90s as paranoid fiction. Now it reads like a documentary.

Paranoid Android, Karma Police, No Surprises — each song is a different angle on the same feeling: the world is accelerating and you're not sure you want to keep up.

I listen to it differently since I started building systems professionally. There's a specific guilt in spending your day automating something and then going home to this record.
