Stairway to Heaven gets all the attention. But the real magic on this record is in the first three tracks. When the Levee Breaks alone is worth the price of admission — Bonham's drums recorded in a stairwell, hitting like thunder.

If you've only ever listened to this album in the background, do yourself a favour and listen properly. Headphones, no distractions.
