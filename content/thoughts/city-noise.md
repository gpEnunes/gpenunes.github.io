There's traffic outside. Not the daytime kind, the late night kind — sparse, rhythmic, almost meditative. A motorbike. A distant conversation. Someone's music bleeding from a window two floors up.

I've been living in cities long enough to tune all of this out. But tonight I'm not tuning it out. I'm listening to it like it's saying something.

I don't know what it's saying. But I like that it's there.
