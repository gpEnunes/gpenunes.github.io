---
title: Finding rhythm in the chaos
description: Life moves fast but there's a certain rhythm to it all if you know where to listen.
date: 2026-04-14
mood: reflective
---

There's something about the end of the day — when the noise settles — that makes everything feel more manageable. The city doesn't slow down, but you can choose to.

I've been thinking a lot about pace lately. Not just in work, but in everything. Cooking slowly. Reading without a target. Listening to a full album without skipping.

There's value in things that take time.
